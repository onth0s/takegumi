# The Swallow and the Sword Saint

[[1]]

The hills outside Beauclair smell of lavender and old stone. The kind of place where retired knights come to drink themselves to philosophy. Nobody retires here to become dangerous.

Ciri had been told he was somewhere in the city outskirts. The Ducal Guard had been less helpful than usual — they'd gone quiet in the specific way men go quiet when the subject embarrasses them.

Over a glass of bitter cider in a lakeside tavern, she had heard the whispers of a "foreign sword-master" living like a stray fox.

The nobles call him "Sensei", the peasants call him "That Weird Hermit Who Talks to Trees", and the knights resent him because he disarmed the entire Ducal Guard using a wooden spoon. 

The guards insist it was at most five men who'd had too much to drink, and that he used a masterfully crafted dueling weapon, something he called a "bokken" — a strange curved practice sword he carries everywhere he's not allowed steel. 

[[2]]

Intrigued, Ciri sought him out. Partly to learn, partly to test herself. 

She found him the way you find a stone in a stream. Not because it moves — because everything else does. He was sitting beneath a gnarled oak in a sunlit clearing, eyes closed.

His lips moved slightly, mentally rehearsing the final lines of a treatise on solitude he would call "The Way of Walking Alone". He seemed less like a warrior and more like a man trying to decide if teaching students for coin was worth the interruption to his peace.

[[3]]

_Musashi_ (not looking up): You've been standing at the treeline for four minutes.

Ciri's hand left the hilt of her sword. She didn't like being read so easily. It made her feel like prey, and Ciri had spent a very long time ensuring that wasn't the case.

_Ciri_: I was observing.

_Musashi_: You were deciding whether I was worth your time. Conclusion?

She stepped out of the treeline, her boots crunching on the dry earth.

_Ciri_, stepping into the clearing: Still forming.

_Musashi_: Hm.

Ciri crossed her arms, the leather of her bracers creaking. 

_Ciri_: So you're the legendary 'Spoon-Warrior'?

_Musashi_, with his eyes still closed: And you're the "Girl Who Steps Through Walls". Sit. Your feet are too loud.

Ciri didn't sit down, but instead leaned into a nearby old tree.

_Musashi_: You have the wrong rumor. I used a bokken. The spoon was for the soup.

_Ciri_: A wooden sword against Toussaint steel? That's a quick way to get a notched piece of wood.

_Musashi_: A sword is just an extension of the intent. If the intent is sharp, the material is irrelevant. 

_Ciri_: The Guards said—

_Musashi_: The Guards say many things. Mostly to avoid admitting they lost to a man eating lunch.

Musashi opened his eyes, finally acknowledging her presence with more than just words. He looked at the way she griped her sword hilt — iron‑hard and ready.

[[4]]

_Musashi_: You hold that blade like it's the only thing keeping you tethered to this world. Relax your hand, Swallow. A sword should be like a bird in your palm — too tight and you crush it, too loose and it flies away. Right now, you're trying to strangle the air itself.

_Ciri_: It's kept me alive across a dozen worlds. I think I'll keep my grip.

_Musashi_: Survival is a habit, not a style. You move with the weight of someone being chased, yet you are the one who sought me out. That is a contradiction. A warrior with a conflicted heart is just a corpse looking for a place to fall.

_Ciri_, bristling: I came to spar.  

_Musashi_, finally glancing at her: Hm. You've got *hunger* in your eyes. Alright. 
But no magic.

_Ciri_, smirking: No promises.

### **The Duel: Instinct vs. Art**  

**Round 1**: Ciri attacks **without teleporting**—Musashi parries effortlessly, his wooden sword a blur. He critiques her stance mid-swing: *"Your hips are tense. You fight like someone who's always running."*  

**Round 2**: Ciri **blinks** behind him—  
- Musashi **barely twists away**, but her blade nicks his shoulder. **First blood in years.**  
- Instead of anger, he **laughs**. *"HAH! So *that's* your 'Way'!"*  
- **Ciri**, panting: *"How did you—?"*  
- **Musashi**, tapping his temple: *"I didn't. My *sword* did. When you vanished, I stopped thinking. The body knows."*  

---

### **The Lesson: "Cutting Without a Sword"**  

- Musashi sits her down, brews tea (badly), and explains:  
  - *"You rely on your feet too much. A true strike doesn't need movement—just *intent*."*  
  - *"Your 'blinking' is just another step. Predictable, if I *listen*."*  
- To demonstrate, he **closes his eyes** and tells Ciri to strike when ready.  
  - She teleports—**he blocks without looking**, his stick halting her blade an inch from his neck.  
  - **Ciri's mind = blown.**  

---

### **The Wisdom Exchange**  

- **Ciri** admits she's always fighting *toward* something (revenge, duty, survival).  
- **Musashi** scoffs: *"That's why you're sloppy. Fight *as* the fight. No past, no future. Just the cut."*  
- **Ciri**, skeptical: *"That's… annoyingly wise."*  
- **Musashi**, grinning: *"I *know*. It's why people pay me."*  

---

### **The Parting Gift**  

- Musashi gives her a **single page** from his book—a sketch of a swallow mid-flight, captioned:  
  - *"The blade that chases the wind will never catch it."*  
- **Ciri's Response**: She *"accidentally"* leaves a bottle of Beauclair's finest wine in his hut.  
- **Musashi's Reaction**: *"Huh. Maybe I *should* teach nobles' kids more often."*
